
COMPANY CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

1. ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT

Customers can create an account using their email address.
If a customer forgets their password, they can use the
Forgot Password option on the login page.

For account access problems, customers should contact
the support team through the official support channel.

2. PAYMENT POLICY

Customers can pay using the payment methods supported
by the company checkout system.

If money is deducted but an order is not confirmed,
customers should contact support and provide their
transaction reference through a secure channel.

3. REFUND POLICY

Refund requests must be submitted through customer support.

The support team reviews each request according to the
company's refund policy.

Refund processing time depends on the payment provider
and the outcome of the review.

4. GENERAL SUPPORT

Customers can contact the customer support department
for account, payment and general inquiries.

The support team aims to provide clear and helpful guidance.


5. LEAVE POLICY

Annual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 20 annual leave days per year.

Sick Leave:
Employees are entitled to 10 sick leave days per year.

Casual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 5 casual leave days per year.

Applying for Leave:
Employees must submit a leave request to their manager
for approval.

Leave Approval:
Leave requests are subject to manager approval and
company requirements.

Unused Leaves:
Unused annual leave can be carried forward according
to company policy.
